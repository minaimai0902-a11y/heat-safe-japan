REMEMBER THESE 4 ACTIONS

Drink. Rest. Cool. Ask for help.

If you start to feel unwell, stop what you are doing. Don't try to push through it.

💧
01

DRINK

Take frequent sips of water. If you sweat a lot, an electrolyte drink can help replace lost salts.

Find drinks at vending machines or convenience stores.
⛱️
02

REST

Move into shade or an air-conditioned place. Take breaks before you feel exhausted.

A shop or station may offer a cooler indoor space.
🧊
03

COOL

Cool your body with a wet towel or cold pack, especially around your neck, armpits and groin.

Loosen extra clothing and use a fan if available.
🆘
04

ASK FOR HELP

Tell a friend, staff member or passer-by as soon as you feel unwell.

For an emergency in Japan, call 119.

NOTICE THE SIGNS

Feeling off? Act early.

Heat illness can get worse quickly. Stop, move somewhere cool and ask for help if you notice symptoms.

!Dizziness or light-headedness
!Headache or nausea
!Unusual weakness or heavy sweating
!Muscle cramps or feeling faint
!NEED HELP NOW?

Someone may have heat illness?

Act quickly and stay with them. If they seem seriously unwell, call an ambulance immediately.

1

Move to a cooler place

Get them out of direct sun and into shade or an air-conditioned room.

2

Cool the body

Loosen clothing and cool the skin with cold wet towels or ice packs.

3

Check if they can drink safely

Only if fully awake and able to swallow, offer small sips of water or an electrolyte drink.

AMBULANCE IN JAPAN

119

Call if they are confused, collapse, have seizures, are hard to wake, cannot drink, or seem seriously unwell.

☎Call 119

If calling is difficult, ask someone nearby to call for you.

Show this message to someone nearby

I may have heat illness. Please call an ambulance.

You can show them this screen if you need help communicating.

Do not give drinks to someone who is confused, unconscious, vomiting or unable to swallow. Do not leave them alone. Call 119 if their condition is severe or worsening.

BEFORE YOU HEAD OUT

Make room for cool-down breaks.

A few small habits can help you enjoy your trip more safely.

  • ✓Check the weather forecast and heat alerts.
  • ✓Carry water and drink regularly, not only when thirsty.
  • ✓Take breaks in a cool indoor place.
  • ✓Avoid strenuous activity during the hottest part of the day.
